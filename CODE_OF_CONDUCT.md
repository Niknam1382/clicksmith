# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Clicksmith's community - issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project
space - to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level,
background, identity, or how they use the software.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and receive feedback constructively; assume good faith.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and that "no" or "not right now" to a request isn't
  personal.
- Respect that Clicksmith is input-automation software: discuss its use honestly, including its
  limits (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and the README's Responsible use section).

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and
any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private report via
[GitHub's "Report content" tool](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
on the relevant comment or issue, or by contacting the maintainers listed in the repository. All
reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion. Maintainers may remove comments, close
issues/PRs, or block contributors who violate this Code of Conduct.
